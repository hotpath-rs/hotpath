# hotpath-cloud pricing

`hotpath-cloud` is priced per repository. Public repositories are free, and `hotpath-cloud` will always offer a free plan for them. A private repository needs a paid plan once its free trial ends.

## Plans

| | Free | Private | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 | $49 / month | $99 / month |
| Repositories | Public | Private | Public or private |
| Benchmarks per repository | 8 | 8 | 32 |
| Reports history | Last 1,000 | Last 1,000 | Last 10,000 |

- **Billing:** prices are in USD, billed monthly per repository, and exclude VAT. Sales tax is added at checkout where it applies. A subscription renews automatically each month until it is cancelled.
- **Free trial:** each private repository gets one 30-day free trial, starting with its first upload. No card is needed and nothing is charged when it ends. After the trial, uploads are refused until the repository has a plan; its reports stay available on the dashboard.
- **Subscribing:** open the repository's page on the [dashboard](https://hotpath.rs/app) and choose a plan. Each plan covers one repository.
- **Payments and refunds:** orders are processed by Paddle, our reseller and Merchant of Record. See [Payments](terms.md#payments), [Subscriptions](terms.md#subscriptions) and [Refunds](terms.md#refunds).

## Limits

- **Benchmarks:** the number of benchmark names a repository can hold. Only a new name counts: an upload under an existing name is always accepted.
- **Reports history:** the most recent reports each repository keeps. Older reports are deleted, except each benchmark's latest report and latest baseline.
